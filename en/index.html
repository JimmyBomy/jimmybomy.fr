Client project · in production · 2026

Amitié Artisan — a web platform for local food producers

The brief

A national network of food businesses (bakeries, butchers, cheese shops, restaurants…) showing where each product comes from and, in real time, where to buy it. Not a brochure site: a platform with accounts and paid subscriptions.

The challenge

Two audiences that must connect: producers present their craft and products, shops declare their outlets and suppliers. All with paid sign-ups and a three-language interface.

What I did

Developed the application for the client: separate sign-up and login flows for shops and producers, member area, invitations between producers and outlets, unlimited products, subscription plans and FR/EN/DE versions.

The result

Live at amitie-artisan.fr with around a hundred listings of producers, makers and shops.
