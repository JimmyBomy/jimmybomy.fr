Privacy policy

In short: I only collect what I need to reply to you, I never sell it, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time. The French version is the legally binding one: Politique de confidentialité.

Data controller

Jimmy BOMY, sole trader (micro-entrepreneur). Contact details are in the legal notice.

What data?

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • Quick quote: your name (optional), your email or phone number, and your answers to the three questions.
  • Technical data linked to sending: IP address (to block abuse, deleted after 30 days) and browser type.

Why?

Only to answer your requests: get back to you, prepare a quote and discuss your project. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps taken at your request (GDPR art. 6.1.b) and the legitimate interest in protecting the form against spam (art. 6.1.f).

Who can access it?

Only me. Messages are stored on the server hosting this site (see the legal notice). They are never sold, rented or used for advertising.

How long?

  • 3 years after our last exchange if the request does not lead to working together, then deleted automatically.
  • If you become a client: for as long as we work together, then for the period required by law for accounting documents (10 years for invoices).
  • IP address: 30 days.

Visit statistics

The site counts visits with a home-made tool, without cookies: only an anonymous fingerprint, recalculated every day and impossible to link to you, is stored with the page viewed. These statistics are deleted after 13 months.

Storage in your browser

Your BomyOS settings (wallpaper, colour, sounds) are kept in your browser (localStorage). They are never sent to me and you can clear them from the Settings app.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare protects and speeds up the site; it processes your IP address to do so and may set a technical security cookie.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces; your browser downloads them from Google's servers.
  • If you choose to contact me on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's privacy policy applies to that conversation.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing. Just write to me through the site's contact form or at the email address in the legal notice, giving the address or number you used. I reply within one month at most.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
